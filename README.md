# Sales, Logistics & Staff Dashboard (Power BI)

A 4-page Power BI report built on a marketplace database (orders, stocks, warehouses, clients, staff).

## Pages
1. **Sales & profit** — total revenue, gross profit, margin, average check, share of returns, by category and order status
2. **Logistics & warehouses** — revenue by warehouse, current stock, inventory turnover, average delivery tariff by shipping method and region
3. **Clients & regions** — number of clients, revenue by region and city, sales volume
4. **Staff & efficiency** — headcount, payroll, average salary, revenue per employee by warehouse
5. **Insights** — conclusions for the business

## Skills shown
Data model with several related tables, calendar table, DAX measures (margin, return rate, turnover, revenue per employee), interactive filters.
